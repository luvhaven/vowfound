# VowFound operating playbook

Draft operating procedures for owner adoption. Named owners, service levels, and capacity must be confirmed before being promised publicly.

## Lead to engagement

1. Review a consultation request; assign an owner and record received time.
2. Confirm coverage, service availability, and the client's preferred timezone.
3. Offer actual appointment slots. Mark booked only after both parties confirm.
4. Send the agenda, practitioner details, fee if applicable, and joining instructions.
5. Record attendance and the agreed next step. Follow up only within the person's communication permissions.

## Assessment and audit

1. Check that the submitted assessment is available to the authorised practitioner.
2. Prepare context and questions; do not treat an automated result as a clinical diagnosis.
3. Deliver the agreed session and written map.
4. Record the delivery date, client acknowledgement, and next review action.
5. Record all preparation, session, administration, and follow-up time for costing.

## Coaching

Document agreed goals and session schedule. After each session record attendance, work assigned, and the next milestone. Track missed sessions and remaining delivery obligations. Refer matters outside the practitioner's competence appropriately.

## Matchmaking

Confirm feasibility and written scope before onboarding. Review essential constraints before preferences. Document the reason for each proposed introduction. Seek both parties' consent before sharing identifying details. Record declines, delivery against the agreement, and follow-up. Do not count a generated shortlist as a completed introduction.

## Customer support

Assign a named owner for support, payments, and safety concerns. Define coverage during absence. Record complaints, refunds, reasons, and resolution dates. Validate public statements about role separation against the actual organisation.

## Weekly commercial review

Review visitors, assessment starts/completions, requests, confirmed consultations, attendance, purchases, delivery hours, refunds, and completed engagements. Segment by programme and acquisition source. Count unique sessions consistently; distinguish requests from bookings and checkout starts from collected revenue.

## Unit economics

Contribution per engagement = collected fee − refunds − payment fees − practitioner cost − variable tools − acquisition cost − other delivery costs.

The $79 audit needs preparation and written-report time included, not only the hour on the call. Decide whether it should stand alone profitably or act as an acquisition offer using observed upgrade rates. Do not assume later upgrades will cover losses.

## Transfer test

Have someone other than the founder run a permitted test enquiry through scheduling and delivery preparation using these procedures. Record questions and update the procedure until the handover works without undocumented founder knowledge.
