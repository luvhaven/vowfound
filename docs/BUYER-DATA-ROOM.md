# Buyer data room preparation

Internal working checklist. No business valuation, customer result, or revenue claim is asserted by this document.

## Financial evidence

Prepare monthly revenue, refunds, processor fees, practitioner costs, acquisition spend, overhead, and profit. Reconcile to processor and bank statements. Separate owner compensation and estimate the cost of replacing the owner's work. Track services paid for but not yet delivered. Keep raw financial records outside the public repository.

Monthly reporting columns: month, currency, collected revenue, refunds, processor fees, delivery labour, marketing, software, other costs, owner replacement cost, operating profit, outstanding delivery obligations.

## Acquisition evidence

Export verified traffic and campaign reports. Document definitions, bot exclusions, attribution limitations, and periods with missing tracking. Reconcile paid engagements to payment records rather than browser success events.

The current event client carries campaign parameters across a tab session. This is session attribution, not cross-device attribution or a complete revenue attribution system. Browser storage loss and tracking restrictions can break continuity.

## Operational evidence

Practitioner agreements, verified qualifications, programme procedures, capacity, fulfilment logs, refund history, complaints process, and hours spent by the owner. Customer and practitioner data access must follow appropriate permissions.

## Asset register

For the domain, logo, code, photographs, fonts, content, social accounts, and software dependencies record: owner, source, licence, transfer restrictions, renewal date, and evidence location. Confirm image licences independently; possession of a file is not proof of rights.

## Technical handover

Document hosting, database migrations, backups, payment providers, email, calendar, monitoring, and admin roles. Store credentials in a password manager, not this file. Test account recovery and restoration. Have the buyer provision their own credentials at handover.

## Listing copy draft

“VowFound is a marriage-readiness and private-matchmaking platform with assessment, client accounts, programme checkout, and administration tools. Included assets: [verified list]. Financial performance: [verified period and figures]. Operating requirements: [actual staffing and owner hours]. Growth opportunities: [clearly labelled hypotheses].”

Never describe programme sales as monthly recurring revenue. Do not list unfilled roles as staff or hypothetical outcomes as case studies. A $500,000 asking price requires supporting financial or strategic evidence; design improvements do not establish that price.

## Launch verification still required

- Confirm production payment configuration; use provider test mode for transaction and webhook tests.
- Confirm successful payment records, cancellations, refunds, and account entitlements.
- Verify scheduling, confirmation, reminders, and email delivery with authorised test recipients.
- Verify role access, privacy controls, and backup restoration.
- Collect actual customer and operational evidence over time.

These are pending evidence tasks, not completed certifications.
